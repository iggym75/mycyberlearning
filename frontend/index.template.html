Privacy notice

My Cyber Learning (mycyberlearning.co.uk) is run by Ian Moore, who is the data controller. Version 2026-11-06.

Daily briefing subscribers

  • What we keep: your email address, and if you give them, your first name, country and topic preferences, plus when and how you agreed.
  • Why: only to send you the briefing you asked for. Lawful basis: your consent. We never sell or share your details, and never use them for advertising.
  • Confirmation: we send one confirmation email, and nothing else until you confirm. If you don't, your address is deleted after 7 days.
  • Unsubscribing: one click in any email. Your details are deleted straight away; we keep only a one-way hash of your address as a record that you opted out. You can subscribe again at any time.
  • No tracking: our emails contain no tracking pixels and no tracked links.

Visitors

  • We count page views by day, section, time-zone region (for example Europe/London), language, device type and referring website. We don't use cookies, we don't store IP addresses or any identifier, and we can't tell individual visitors apart.
  • If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we don't count you at all.
  • Visitors never receive a cookie. The site's administrator signs in with one essential session cookie.
  • Votes are remembered with a random number stored in your browser; on our servers it is kept only as a one-way hash.
  • If you suggest a resource, we keep a one-way hash of your IP address to stop spam, and any email you add is used only to ask about that suggestion. Both are deleted once the suggestion is reviewed, or after 90 days.

Experts we list

  • What we show: the person's name, their public role, a short description we write ourselves, the security topics they cover, and links to profiles and sites they publish themselves. Where they have one, we also show the profile picture they chose for a public profile of their own (such as Bluesky, Mastodon, YouTube or their website), kept as a small copy on our site so your browser doesn't contact those services. We don't copy their posts or collect anything else about them.
  • Why: to help people find respected voices in security. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in pointing learners to people who share their expertise publicly, limited to professional information they have made public.
  • Corrections and removal: if you're listed and want a link or picture changed, your picture taken down, or to be removed altogether, email privacy@mycyberlearning.co.uk. We act on removal requests promptly, without needing a reason.

Who processes data for us

Cloudflare (hosting and database; the database is stored in the EU), Resend (delivering the briefing emails) and GitHub (building the site). Some of these companies are based in the US; we rely on their data protection terms for any transfer. Anthropic's Claude writes the briefing summaries from public podcast notes; no subscriber or visitor data is ever sent to it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion, or withdraw consent at any time. Contact privacy@mycyberlearning.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
